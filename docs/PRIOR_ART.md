# Prior Art and Novelty Positioning

SatQuery AI sits in a fast-moving area. Several existing systems already solve important subproblems, so novelty must be framed carefully.

## Important prior systems

### GeoChat (CVPR 2024)

A grounded remote-sensing VLM supporting tasks such as VQA, image/region captioning, scene classification and referring/grounded outputs.

### TEOChat (ICLR 2025)

A vision-language assistant focused on temporal Earth Observation data, with strong single-image and temporal capabilities.

### EarthDial (CVPR 2025)

A conversational Earth Observation model spanning multiple sensors/modalities and tasks including captioning, VQA, reasoning and grounding.

### ChangeChat (2024)

An interactive bitemporal remote-sensing change-analysis model supporting change captioning, quantification and localization.

### DeltaVLM (2025)

An interactive change-analysis VLM emphasizing instruction-guided perception of bitemporal differences.

## Novelty assessment

A **generic remote-sensing chatbot** is not highly novel on its own. The stronger positioning is:

1. integrated SIH-ready orchestration across the required capability families;
2. deterministic GIS measurement attached to model answers;
3. transparent evidence objects and spatial provenance;
4. sensor-quality-aware optical–SAR arbitration;
5. contradiction and registration checks;
6. calibrated abstention.

The research novelty should therefore live in **EvidenceFuse-RS**, not in claiming that conversational remote-sensing VLMs themselves are new.
